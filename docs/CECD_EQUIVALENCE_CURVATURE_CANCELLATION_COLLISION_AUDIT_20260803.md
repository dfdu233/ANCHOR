# CECD Equivalence-Curvature Cancellation collision audit

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Scope:** literature and protocol audit only; no GPU runs and no inspection of
sealed outcomes.  The candidate is evaluated only as a post-CECD-gate causal
intervention.

## 1. Executive verdict

The proposed correction

\[
\Delta h=h_{11}-h_{10}-h_{01}+h_{00},\qquad
\delta_{ij}=-s_{ij}\Delta h/4,
\quad s=(+1,-1,-1,+1)
\]

is the unique minimum-Euclidean-norm projection of a vector-valued `2 x 2`
table onto its additive subspace.  Its mathematics is classical factorial
ANOVA/Walsh decomposition, and the generic act of projecting activations away
from a contrast or counterfactual subspace is already heavily occupied by
activation steering and concept-erasure work.  **It is therefore a no-go as a
standalone algorithmic novelty claim.**

It remains worth retaining as a **conditional causal probe inside CECD**.  Its
specific value is that, unlike full-orbit averaging, it preserves both admitted
render and prompt main effects and removes only their interaction.  Unlike
Treble, the direction is an instance-level second-order residual generated by
two independently clinician-admitted nuisance equivalences, not a globally
fitted modality-dependence direction.  That scientific delta survives only if
the intervention selectively rescues the erroneous joint cell, has negligible
effect on both marginal cells and clear cases, adds information beyond Treble,
and replicates across architectures.

The honest name is **interaction-component ablation** or
**additive-subspace causal projection**.  “Equivalence-Curvature Cancellation”
may be a descriptive experiment label, but not a claim that the projection
operator itself is new.

## 2. Exact geometry

Order the four activation vectors as
`H=(h00,h01,h10,h11)` and let the interaction operator be

\[
C=[I,-I,-I,I], \qquad CH=\Delta h.
\]

The constrained problem is

\[
\min_\delta \sum_{ij}\|\delta_{ij}\|_2^2
\quad \text{s.t.}\quad C(H+\delta)=0.
\]

Because `CC^T=4I`, the Euclidean orthogonal projection gives

\[
\delta=-C^T(CC^T)^{-1}CH,
\]

hence `delta_ij=-s_ij Delta h/4`.  It follows immediately that

\[
\Delta h'=\Delta h-\tfrac14(1+1+1+1)\Delta h=0.
\]

The correction has zero sum within every row and every column.  It therefore
preserves the grand mean, both render marginal means and both prompt marginal
means.  This is exactly the orthogonal projection onto

\[
h_{ij}=\mu+\alpha_i+\beta_j,
\]

the additive subspace of a balanced `2 x 2` factorial.  Functional-ANOVA work
already formalizes canonical separation of marginal and interaction effects
([Lengerich et al., AISTATS 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/lengerich20a.html));
latent decomposition into additive and nonlinear interaction components is
also established
([Martens et al., AISTATS 2020](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/martens20a.html)).

For the canonical cell,

\[
h'_{00}=h_{00}-\Delta h/4
=\bar h_{0\cdot}+\bar h_{\cdot0}-\bar h_{\cdot\cdot}.
\]

Thus the proposed hidden-state operator is the exact vector analogue of the
score-level additive-orbit projection already preregistered in
`MEDICAL_VLM_MECHANISM_FRONTIER_SCAN_20260802.md`.  The hidden interaction
residual itself was also already specified in
`MECHANISM_COLLISION_AUDIT_CECD_SPECIFICITY_20260802.md`.  This is strong
outcome-blind preregistration, but it prevents presenting the renamed operator
as a newly discovered method.

## 4. Relation to orbit averaging

Full-orbit averaging maps all four cells to their grand mean.  It projects onto
the constant subspace and destroys both factor main effects.  ECC projects only
onto the additive subspace:

```text
four-cell representation
        |
        +-- full-orbit average --> grand mean only
        |
        +-- ECC ----------------> grand mean + render main effect
                                  + prompt main effect
                                  - interaction
```

This gives ECC independent **mechanistic** value: it can test whether the joint
non-additive component, rather than either marginal sensitivity, causes the
clinical error.  It provides no automatic **utility** advantage.  A nonlinear
decoder can benefit more from the lower-variance grand-mean ensemble, and a
hidden interaction may encode legitimate fusion.  ECC must therefore beat
full-orbit averaging at the same four target calls, fixed claim count/coverage,
and matched output length.  If both methods help equally, the parsimonious
explanation is ensembling or smoothing rather than CECD-specific correction.

## 5. Relation to Treble

The defensible distinction is about identification, not the algebra of
steering:

- Treble constructs visual, textual and cross-modal Natural Direct Effect
  directions using external perturbations/degradations and global PCA, then
  adjusts modality dependence at test time.
- ECC computes a within-example Walsh interaction from a `2 x 2` orbit whose
  two axes have first been admitted as proposition- and visibility-preserving.
  Its scientific null is additive equivalence: the joint residual should not
  change reader-grounded clinical truth.
- Treble is amortized after calibration; exact per-instance ECC costs four
  target branches.  They must be plotted on a latency/quality Pareto frontier,
  not falsely described as compute-matched.

ECC has independent value over Treble only if the residual predicts held-out
clinical error beyond clean margin, both main effects, entropy and Treble's
scores; then its selective ablation must improve the joint cell while leaving
clean and marginal cells unchanged.  If a pooled interaction direction is
learned across examples and merely projected out, the method collapses toward
Treble/CIPHER/ASD and the novelty delta largely disappears.

The local Treble audit records unresolved source-semantics, licensing and Hulu
token-transport blockers. Exact paper-native Treble therefore remains blocked.
The only admissible fallback is the frozen dual-semantics common-protocol
envelope: proceedings-faithful and released-source-faithful variants are both
evaluated, neither is called exact, and ECC must beat their stronger observed
result as well as full-orbit averaging in both models. A single blended or
convenient surrogate remains only a diagnostic control.

## 6. Fatal identifiability and implementation risks

1. **Nonlinearity is not pathology.** A model may use a non-additive internal
   code and still produce the same correct clinical answer in all four cells.
   Nonzero `Delta h` alone proves nothing.
2. **Layer cancellation is not output cancellation.** The downstream nonlinear
   map can recreate an interaction even when `Delta h_l'=0`.  Both post-hook and
   output-level interactions must be measured.
3. **Euclidean minimum norm is coordinate-dependent.** The formula is minimal
   only under an isotropic activation metric, not under output Jacobian,
   covariance-whitened, Fisher, or clinical-loss geometry.  Do not claim
   minimum clinical distortion.
4. **Norm restoration conflicts with exact cancellation.** Independently
   rescaling each corrected cell generally makes `Delta h'` nonzero.  Exact
   projection and per-cell norm restoration cannot both be claimed without a
   new constrained solve; report both values if a norm control is used.
5. **Token alignment is mandatory.** Different prompt lengths make whole
   sequence tensors incomparable.  The readout must be frozen before outcomes,
   such as an aligned last-question-token state or teacher-forced claim token.
6. **OE branches diverge.** Free-running four-cell answers no longer share token
   identities.  OE validation should generate candidates once and teacher-force
   aligned atomic claims across the four cells; otherwise `Delta h` has no
   coherent tokenwise meaning.
7. **Single-cell intervention is asymmetric.** Applying only
   `h00 <- h00-Delta h/4` is the projected canonical cell, but does not let one
   empirically verify the full corrected orbit.  For the causal study, cache and
   score all four corrected cells even if deployment reports only the canonical
   one.
8. **Activation-steering specificity is fragile.** Recent evaluations show that
   apparently effective steering can lack specificity
   ([EACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.268/)); the clean,
   marginal, prompt-only, random-direction and clear-finding controls are
   mechanism requirements, not optional ablations.

## 7. Outcome-blind decisive experiment

This experiment is authorized **only after** the CECD human-admission and
behavioral gates pass.  Freeze the layer and readout using dev data; do not scan
test layers for the best mitigation.

For identical locked examples, compare:

1. original canonical cell;
2. exact four-cell additive projection, reporting both canonical and complete
   corrected orbit;
3. full-orbit averaging at the same four target calls;
4. render-only and prompt-only averaging controls;
5. same-norm random direction;
6. sign-permuted or image-permuted interaction residual;
7. matched-energy main-effect removal;
8. faithful Treble and its component controls once the reproduction gate is
   resolved.

The causal claim passes only if all of the following hold:

- `Delta h_l` is removed at the hook and the independently measured output
  interaction falls;
- reader-grounded error is selectively rescued in the erroneous joint cell;
- both marginal cells, the clean cell, prompt-only PSF cases and clear findings
  change negligibly;
- fixed-`K` OE precision improves without lower recall, shorter answers, higher
  refusal, or blanket hedging;
- the effect beats full-orbit averaging with a paired image-cluster bootstrap
  interval excluding zero;
- the result replicates in a second architecture and across a majority of
  admitted finding/transform families.

Falsifiers are decisive:

- if output interaction reappears immediately downstream, the selected layer
  is not a causally sufficient localization;
- if clean or marginal cells improve similarly, the intervention is generic
  calibration/smoothing;
- if orbit averaging matches it, no mechanism-specific mitigation remains;
- if only a pooled/global direction works, the contribution belongs to generic
  steering rather than instance-level CECD;
- if admission fails, the interaction is ordinary signal change and the entire
  equivalence interpretation is invalid.

## 8. Reviewer-style score and decision

### Standalone ECC method

| Dimension | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Importance | 1/3 | An activation correction operator alone does not establish the clinical mechanism |
| Tractability | 2/3 | Algebra is exact, but four aligned branches and OE synchronization are costly |
| Novelty | 0/3 | Direct factorial-projection mathematics; high collision with counterfactual activation projection |
| Evidence | 2/3 | Clean falsifiable tests exist, but no authorized outcomes yet |

**Decision: prune as a standalone method contribution.**

### Nested CECD causal probe

Potentially high mechanistic value, but currently **conditional/not authorized**:
the human equivalence and behavioral gates have not been assumed passed in this
outcome-blind audit.  If those gates pass, interaction-component ablation is the
right decisive next experiment because it distinguishes joint composition
failure from marginal sensitivity and four-pass smoothing.  Until then it
should remain a preregistered probe, not the paper title or claimed solution.
